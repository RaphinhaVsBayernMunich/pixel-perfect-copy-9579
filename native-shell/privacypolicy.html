How QuestOS uses health data

Connecting Health Connect is optional. QuestOS requests read access to steps, sleep and exercise. It does not request permission to write health records.

When you choose Read Health Connect, QuestOS reads the last 30 days on your device and prepares daily totals. Only after you choose Save this summary are daily dates, steps, sleep minutes and workout minutes sent to your signed-in QuestOS account and stored in its Supabase database. Raw samples are not uploaded. Health data is not included in AI Coach requests.

You can replace or clear the saved summary in the Premium workspace. Downgrading preserves your data; you can still export or clear stored health data in Settings. Revoking access in Health Connect stops future reads but does not remove a previously saved summary.

This feature is for personal activity review, not medical advice. See QuestOS Settings → Privacy for the full privacy policy and contact details.
